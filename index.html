Frequently Asked Questions

What is carnosine gel and how is it different from other products?
Carnosine gel is a topical product that delivers carnosine directly through the skin to muscle tissue. Carnosine is a naturally occurring dipeptide that buffers lactic acid during exercise — delaying fatigue and reducing the burn. LactiGo is the only patented carnosine gel on the market (US Patent 10,973,868), developed over 10 years by a two-sport Olympian and clinical nutrition expert.
When should I apply LactiGo?
Apply LactiGo to clean, dry skin 15–30 minutes before training or competition. Massage into the target muscle area — quads, hamstrings, calves, shoulders — until fully absorbed. It dries fast with no sticky residue. You can also apply post-workout to support recovery.
Is carnosine gel better than beta-alanine or oral carnosine supplements?
For most people, yes. Oral beta-alanine causes a tingling sensation (paresthesia) and must be converted to carnosine through a slow metabolic process. Oral carnosine is largely broken down in the gut before reaching muscles. LactiGo delivers carnosine topically — directly to muscles — with no tingling, no GI issues, and results within 45 minutes.
Can carnosine gel help with healthy aging?
Yes — and this is one of its most compelling use cases. Muscle carnosine levels decline by up to 63% between ages 20 and 70. Published research shows that restoring carnosine levels improves exercise capacity most in older adults. Many LactiGo users are active people in their 40s–60s who want to train hard and recover properly.
Is LactiGo safe for drug-tested athletes?
Yes. LactiGo is Informed Sport certified — independently tested for banned substances and safe for athletes in all drug-tested sports including Olympic, professional league, and sanctioned competition use.
