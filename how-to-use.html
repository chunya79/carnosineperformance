Practical Use Guide

How to approach a new topical gel

Use the current package directions as the authority. This guide helps you introduce the product carefully without turning an old web instruction into a substitute label.

Before your first application

Read the current label.
Confirm intended areas, amount, timing, warnings, storage and whether directions differ between regular and menthol formats.
Check the full ingredients.
Look for known sensitivities. The label on the supplied product is more current than a third-party ingredient summary.
Consider a small-area test.
A cautious introduction can help identify irritation before wider use. Stop if an unwanted reaction develops.
Do not mask a problem.
Persistent pain, swelling, weakness or an injury deserves suitable professional assessment rather than repeated topical application.

Keep your evaluation realistic

Change one thing

If possible, avoid adding several recovery products at once. Otherwise it becomes difficult to know what affected your experience.

Track the same outcome

Record the exercise, area used, timing and a consistent measure. A general impression is more vulnerable to expectation and day-to-day variation.

Separate sensation from outcome

Cooling or tingling is a sensory response. It does not by itself show improved recovery, injury resolution or enhanced performance.

Medical boundary: LactiGo is not presented here as a treatment for injury, disease or persistent pain. Ask an appropriate health professional when symptoms or suitability are uncertain.

Which format should you check?

The official shop may offer regular gel, menthol gel, travel packets and bundles. Compare formula, size and intended use—not just price per container.
